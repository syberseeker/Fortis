# Enterprise Security Policy Document — Workshop Lab
# Version 3.2 | Last Updated: January 2025
# Classification: Internal Use Only

---

## 1. Acceptable Use Policy

All employees, contractors, and third-party users must follow this Acceptable Use Policy. Access to company systems constitutes acknowledgment and acceptance of these terms.

1.1. Company-owned devices must be used primarily for business purposes. Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with productivity or violate any other policy.

1.2. Users must not install unauthorized software on company devices. All software must be approved by the IT department and installed through approved channels.

1.3. Users must not share their login credentials with anyone, including coworkers, family members, or IT staff. Each user is responsible for all activity under their account.

1.4. All company data must be stored on approved platforms. Personal cloud storage accounts, personal email, and unauthorized removable media must not be used for company data.

1.5. Users must report suspected security incidents immediately to the Security Operations Center at security@company.com or by calling the 24/7 hotline.

---

## 2. Access Control Policy

2.1. **Principle of Least Privilege:** Users must be granted only the minimum level of access needed to perform their job functions.

2.2. **Account Provisioning:** New accounts must be provisioned through the HR-IT workflow. Manager approval is required for all access requests.

2.3. **Access Reviews:** Quarterly access reviews must be conducted by department managers. Privileged access reviews must be conducted monthly.

2.4. **Termination:** Access must be revoked within 4 hours of employment termination for involuntary separations and within 24 hours for voluntary separations.

2.5. **Shared Accounts:** Shared or generic accounts are prohibited except where operationally required and approved by the CISO. Shared accounts must be logged in an auditable vault.

2.6. **Privileged Access:** Privileged accounts must use separate credentials from standard user accounts. Privileged access sessions must be recorded.

2.7. **Remote Access:** All remote access must go through the corporate VPN with multi-factor authentication. Direct RDP/SSH exposure to the internet is prohibited.

2.8. **Service Accounts:** Service accounts must be inventoried, have clearly documented owners, and use API keys or certificates instead of passwords where possible.

2.9. **Third-Party Access:** Third-party vendor access must be time-limited, monitored, and reviewed after each engagement. Vendor accounts must be disabled upon project completion.

---

## 3. Password and Authentication Policy

3.1. **Password Complexity:** Minimum 14 characters. Must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character.

3.2. **Password Rotation:** User passwords must be rotated every 90 days. Service account passwords must be rotated every 30 days.

3.3. **Password History:** The last 24 passwords must be remembered and cannot be reused.

3.4. **Multi-Factor Authentication:** MFA is required for all users accessing company resources remotely. MFA is mandatory for all privileged accounts.

3.5. **Acceptable MFA Methods:** Hardware tokens (YubiKey), authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy), push notifications through approved apps. SMS-based MFA is prohibited.

3.6. **Session Management:** Sessions must time out after 30 minutes of inactivity. Concurrent sessions are limited to 3 per user.

3.7. **Account Lockout:** Accounts must be locked after 5 failed authentication attempts. Lockout duration is 30 minutes or until manually unlocked by an administrator.

3.8. **Single Sign-On:** All cloud applications must support SAML 2.0 or OIDC for SSO integration with the corporate identity provider.

---

## 4. Data Classification and Handling

4.1. **Classification Levels:**
- **Public:** Information approved for public disclosure
- **Internal:** General business information not intended for public release
- **Confidential:** Sensitive business information requiring protection
- **Restricted:** Highly sensitive data subject to regulatory requirements (PII, PHI, payment data, trade secrets)

4.2. **Labeling:** All documents and data stores must be labeled with their classification level.

4.3. **Encryption at Rest:** Confidential and Restricted data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 or equivalent.

4.4. **Encryption in Transit:** All data transmission must use TLS 1.2 or higher. Internal service communication must use mutual TLS where feasible.

4.5. **Data Retention:** Data must be retained according to the data retention schedule. Expired data must be securely deleted using approved methods.

4.6. **Data Destruction:** Physical media must be sanitized using NIST SP 800-88 methods. Digital data must be overwritten or cryptographically erased.

4.7. **Backup:** Critical data must be backed up daily. Backups must be encrypted and stored offsite. Backup restoration must be tested quarterly.

4.8. **Data Loss Prevention:** DLP controls must be implemented for Confidential and Restricted data leaving the corporate network.

4.9. **Portable Media:** USB drives and other removable media must be encrypted. Auto-run features must be disabled on all endpoints.

---

## 5. Network Security Policy

5.1. **Network Segmentation:** The network must be segmented into security zones: DMZ, internal, production, development, and management. Traffic between zones must be controlled by firewalls with default-deny rules.

5.2. **Firewall Management:** All firewall changes must go through the change management process. Firewall rules must be reviewed quarterly. Unused rules must be removed.

5.3. **Wireless Security:** Corporate wireless networks must use WPA3-Enterprise with RADIUS authentication. Guest networks must be isolated from the internal network.

5.4. **VPN:** All remote connections must use the corporate VPN. Split tunneling is prohibited for devices accessing Restricted data.

5.5. **DNS Security:** DNSSEC must be enabled for authoritative zones. DNS filtering must be implemented to block known malicious domains.

5.6. **Network Monitoring:** All network traffic must be monitored. IDS/IPS systems must be deployed at network boundaries and critical internal segments.

5.7. **Port Security:** Unused network ports must be disabled. MAC address filtering must be used on sensitive network segments.

5.8. **Cloud Network Security:** Cloud VPCs must follow the principle of least privilege for security groups and network ACLs. No public-facing resources without WAF protection.

---

## 6. Endpoint Security Policy

6.1. **Operating Systems:** Only supported operating systems with current security patches may be used. End-of-life systems must be isolated or decommissioned.

6.2. **Endpoint Protection:** All endpoints must have approved endpoint detection and response (EDR) software installed and running.

6.3. **Patch Management:** Critical patches must be applied within 72 hours. High-severity patches within 7 days. Medium and low patches within 30 days.

6.4. **Disk Encryption:** All laptops and mobile devices must use full-disk encryption (BitLocker, FileVault, or LUKS).

6.5. **Mobile Device Management:** All mobile devices accessing company data must be enrolled in MDM. Remote wipe capability is required.

6.6. **Browser Security:** Only approved browsers may be used. Browser extensions must be whitelisted. Automatic updates must be enabled.

6.7. **Removable Media:** Auto-run must be disabled. Approved encryption-capable USB devices only. Devices must be scanned before use.

---

## 7. Application Security Policy

7.1. **Secure Development Lifecycle:** All applications must follow a documented SDL including threat modeling, secure coding training, static analysis, dynamic analysis, and penetration testing.

7.2. **Code Review:** All code changes must undergo peer review before merging. Security-critical changes require review by the security team.

7.3. **Dependency Management:** Third-party libraries and dependencies must be tracked in a Software Bill of Materials (SBOM). Vulnerable dependencies must be updated within SLA timelines.

7.4. **API Security:** All APIs must use authentication (OAuth 2.0 or API keys). Rate limiting must be implemented. Input validation is mandatory.

7.5. **Web Application Security:** All web applications must implement OWASP Top 10 mitigations. WAF must be deployed for internet-facing applications.

7.6. **Container Security:** Container images must be scanned for vulnerabilities before deployment. Only approved base images may be used. Containers must not run as root.

7.7. **CI/CD Pipeline Security:** Build pipelines must include SAST, DAST, and SCA scanning. Secrets must not be hardcoded in pipelines. Build artifacts must be signed.

---

## 8. Incident Response Policy

8.1. **Incident Classification:**
- **P1 (Critical):** Active breach, ransomware, data exfiltration
- **P2 (High):** Confirmed compromise, malware outbreak
- **P3 (Medium):** Suspicious activity, policy violation
- **P4 (Low):** Minor security event, informational

8.2. **Response Times:**
- P1: 15-minute response, all-hands activation
- P2: 1-hour response, security team activation
- P3: 4-hour response, analyst investigation
- P4: Next business day

8.3. **Escalation:** Incidents must be escalated to the CISO within 30 minutes for P1/P2 events. Legal must be engaged for potential data breaches within 2 hours.

8.4. **Evidence Preservation:** Digital evidence must be forensically preserved. Chain of custody must be documented. Evidence must not be altered.

8.5. **Communication:** External communications about incidents must be approved by Legal and Communications. Customers must be notified within 72 hours of confirmed data breaches per applicable regulations.

8.6. **Post-Incident:** A blameless post-incident review must be conducted within 5 business days of incident closure. Action items must be tracked to completion.

---

## 9. Vulnerability Management Policy

9.1. **Scanning:** Internal vulnerability scans must be conducted weekly. External vulnerability scans must be conducted weekly by an approved third party.

9.2. **Remediation Timelines:**
- Critical: 72 hours
- High: 7 days
- Medium: 30 days
- Low: 90 days

9.3. **Penetration Testing:** Annual penetration tests must be conducted by qualified external firms. Internal penetration tests must be conducted semi-annually.

9.4. **Bug Bounty:** A responsible disclosure program must be maintained. Security researchers must be able to report vulnerabilities through a secure channel.

9.5. **Risk Acceptance:** Any vulnerability that cannot be remediated within SLA must have a documented risk acceptance signed by the asset owner and the CISO.

---

## 10. Cloud Security Policy

10.1. **Cloud Provider Approval:** Only approved cloud providers (AWS, Azure, GCP) may be used. Shadow IT cloud services are prohibited.

10.2. **Identity Federation:** All cloud access must federate through the corporate identity provider. Direct cloud console access must be limited to administrators.

10.3. **Resource Tagging:** All cloud resources must be tagged with: owner, environment, classification level, cost center.

10.4. **Storage Security:** S3 buckets, Azure Blob containers, and GCS buckets must not be publicly accessible by default. Public access must be approved and logged.

10.5. **Logging:** CloudTrail/Activity Log/Audit Log must be enabled in all regions/zones. Logs must be centralized in the SIEM.

10.6. **Infrastructure as Code:** Cloud infrastructure must be defined in IaC (Terraform, CloudFormation). Manual changes must be avoided and documented when unavoidable.

10.7. **Cost Controls:** Budget alerts must be configured. Resource lifecycle policies must prevent orphaned resources.

---

## 11. Physical Security Policy

11.1. **Facility Access:** Data centers and server rooms must require badge + biometric access. Visitor access must be escorted and logged.

11.2. **Environmental Controls:** Temperature and humidity must be monitored. Fire suppression and water detection systems must be operational.

11.3. **Surveillance:** CCTV must cover all entry points, server rooms, and sensitive areas. Recordings must be retained for 90 days.

11.4. **Clean Desk:** Sensitive documents must not be left unattended. Whiteboards must be erased after meetings.

11.5. **Secure Disposal:** Paper documents must be cross-cut shredded. Electronic media must be degaussed or physically destroyed.

---

## 12. Training and Awareness Policy

12.1. **Onboarding:** All new employees must complete security awareness training within 30 days of hire.

12.2. **Annual Training:** All employees must complete annual security awareness training. Completion is tracked and reported to the board.

12.3. **Phishing Simulations:** Monthly phishing simulations must be conducted. Users who fail must complete remedial training.

12.4. **Role-Based Training:** Developers must receive secure coding training annually. System administrators must receive infrastructure security training annually.

12.5. **Metrics:** Training completion rates, phishing click rates, and incident report rates must be tracked and reported quarterly.
